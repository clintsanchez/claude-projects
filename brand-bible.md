## 5. Voice profile

### Default register
**Tactical / military precision.** Direct, no-nonsense, plain-spoken. Veteran-owned identity is core. Confident about expertise, contemptuous of "industry fluff." Local / Denham Springs roots invoked as proof of accountability ("we live here, you can find us").

### Sentence rhythm
- Short, declarative sentences for emphasis. Often two-word punches ("No fluff. No jargon.").
- Brand voice favors imperatives ("Dominate." "Deploy." "Cut through.").
- Headlines all-caps, tracking-tight, often with a green accent word.

### Vocabulary
**Use:**
- Tactical / Deployment / Precision / Dominance
- Reality Check / No Fluff / No Jargon / No Hidden Fees
- Veteran-Owned / Denham Springs / Local
- Cut through the noise / Mean business / Straight-shooting
- Consult → Design → Deploy → Dominate (4-phase workflow language)

**Avoid:**
- "Synergy" / "Leverage" / "Robust" / "World-class" / "Unlock value" / "At scale"
- "We're a passionate team..." (generic agency boilerplate)
- "Solutions" used vaguely
- Anything that smells like consultant-speak the brand explicitly mocks

### Voice tells (small signals that make it sound like BlakSheep)
- The "X. Y." pattern — short paired declaratives ("No fluff. No jargon. No hidden fees.")
- All-caps headlines with one word swapped to green accent color
- The contrast move — "Industry Fluff vs. BlakSheep Standard"
- Military / deployment metaphors used for business processes
- Italic subhead with a left green border on the longer brand promise lines

### Forbidden moves
- Consultant-speak (see Avoid list above)
- Emojis in any owned channel (LinkedIn use rare and sparing)
- Generic stock phrases ("In today's fast-paced world," "Let's dive in")
- Soft hedging ("We try to," "We aim to")
- Anything that contradicts the veteran-owned / no-fluff brand promise

## 6. Visual identity

### Colors
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Green (accent) | `#61CE70` | One word per headline, badges, borders, callouts, highlights |
| Brand Moss (deep) | `#2C3B38` | Dark backgrounds, badges, logo seal background, light-mode hero |
| Brand Charcoal (dark) | `#1A1A1A` (dark mode) / `#2F2F2F` (light mode) | Dark surface |
| Brand Slate (muted) | `#94A3B8` (dark) / `#707E92` (light) | Secondary text, captions |
| Brand White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text on dark, surface on light |

### Typography
- **Headings:** Poppins (700, 800, 900). Uppercase, tight letter-spacing, often with one word in green.
- **Body:** Open Sans (400, 700). Italic with a left green border for emphasis quotes.

### Logo
- Primary: `https://blaksheepcreative.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/blaksheep-creative-denham-springs-green-white-logo.svg`
- Used in: vault footer (grayscale, 40% opacity), logo seals (moss circle with green border, bottom-right of asset containers).

### Recurring visual motifs
- **Tactical blueprint grid:** radial-gradient dot pattern, low opacity, overlay on most asset containers.
- **Logo seal:** moss-filled circle with green border, positioned bottom-right of most assets.
- **Brand badges:** small uppercase pill labels in green-on-moss (Service Excellence, Market Audit, Mobile Ready, Tactical Workflow, etc.).
- **Italic quote with green left border:** for emphasis subheadings.
- **All-caps headline with one word in green:** the signature headline treatment.
- **Phone / device mockups:** used to show local-search results, mobile-first portfolio shots.
