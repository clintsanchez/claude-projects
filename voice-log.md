# Voice Log

Running record of pieces BlakSheep has shipped. Templates reference the most recent ~10 entries to keep voice drift-free.

## How to add an entry

After publishing a piece (yours or a client's), append a new block at the **top** of the log using the format below. Takes 30 seconds. Re-upload to Claude Projects' knowledge each Friday.

## Entry format

```
### YYYY-MM-DD · [Channel] · [Brand: BlakSheep | client:slug]
**Title/subject:** ...
**Why it worked / note to self:** 1-2 sentences. What's the voice tell you want preserved?

[Paste final published version below — full text]
---
```
